THE TWO QUESTIONS
Where a cluster setting lives, and how you find one in a hundred
QUESTION 1 · APP-17
One record, two views — and the fleet pane is the one that opens it.
A cluster’s settings are not copied into the fleet pane and they do not live in two places. Right-clicking a fleet row opens this pane, scoped to that cluster — the same rows, the same values, edited in the same place. What the fleet row does carry is the consequence of a setting, stated as state: “paused by you at 09:12”, “read-only”, “no metrics source”.

QUESTION 2 · APP-16
You do not browse for a setting. You type at it — in the pane, or from ⌘K.
The twelve groups are a map for the first week, not the way in. The filter searches names, help text, values and aliases, and it keeps matches from other groups visible at the bottom rather than hiding them behind the group you happen to be in. Typing # in the palette searches only settings and jumps straight to the row.
