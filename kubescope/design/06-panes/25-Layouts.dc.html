Layouts
The brief asks where saved layouts live, how you switch between them, and what happens to unsaved changes. The third question is the one worth answering properly: it should not be askable. A tab is a desk and saves itself continuously; a template is a stencil and never changes on its own. Nothing in this product ever loses a layout, and nothing ever asks you to save one.
