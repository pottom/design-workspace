Time travel — and every pane shouting that this is not now
TIME-02 · the most dangerous feature in the product
One colour, reserved for the past, used in six places at once — so no single one of them has to carry the warning alone.
Violet appears nowhere else in the product. It is not a status, not a cluster hue, not an accent. The moment the frame leaves the present, violet enters — on the window edge, in the time bar, on every pane header, on the clock in every row, on the disabled writes, and on the terminal that cannot come with you. Miss one and you have still seen five.
