The palette, and the help
Two layers, one question asked twice: what can I do here? The palette is where you do it, the help is where you read it — so they are built from the same registry, in the same order, and neither is a static list.

THE QUESTION
Verbs, nouns, or both — the brief’s question, answered
Both — but neither one is the ordering. The list is ordered by distance from what you are looking at.
A palette sorted by verb makes you know the word we chose. Sorted by noun it makes you know where the thing lives. Neither is knowable on day one, and with more than a hundred capabilities both lists are too long to scan.
So matching is on both — type log and you get the verb, type checkout and you get the object — and the order is five rings outward from the cursor. The thing under your cursor first, the app in general last.
